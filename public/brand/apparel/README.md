Use the supplied artwork as-is. The logo files have transparent backgrounds and must not be redrawn, recolored, cropped, stretched, or given added effects.

## Production notes

- Full mark: keep at least 0.5 in / 13 mm of clear space on all sides.
- iQ badge: use at least 0.9 in / 23 mm tall for print; for embroidery, ask the shop to confirm the tennis-ball seam remains legible at the selected thread count.
- Keep white artwork on dark garments and the navy artwork on light garments. Do not place the full lockup over a busy photo or pattern.
- The supplied masters are high-resolution raster artwork. These exports remove only invisible/near-invisible export residue, preserving the approved logo geometry. Have the printer make a physical test before a full run: the source's visible raster texture cannot be converted into a true vector master without the original brand artwork.

Brand colors: navy `#06172F`, green `#9BE11D`, white `#FFFFFF`.
