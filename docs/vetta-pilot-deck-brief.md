# TenAceIQ x Vetta Sports Pilot Deck Brief

This is the approved six-slide story for the client-facing Vetta deck. It keeps the existing TenAceIQ/Vetta visual source, uses the Vetta logo only as a prospective branded example, and does not position TenAceIQ as a booking, registration, or payment system.

## Slide 1 — A connected tennis experience for Vetta

**Headline:** More tennis. Less club chaos.

**Support:** TenAceIQ connects Vetta players, coaches, clinics, teams, leagues, and tournaments in one branded tennis experience.

**Visual:** Vetta logo + TenAceIQ lockup, one strong current Club or mobile workspace image, no feature grid.

## Slide 2 — The work is already happening. It is just spread out.

**Headline:** One player journey currently crosses too many tools.

Show one simple path:

1. Player joins a clinic.
2. Coach plans the next step.
3. Player follows through between sessions.
4. Player joins a team, league, or tournament.
5. Staff and captains coordinate in separate texts, files, and pages.

**TenAceIQ value:** Keep the tennis context connected without replacing the systems Vetta already uses for court booking, registration, or payments.

## Slide 3 — The Vetta-branded experience

**Headline:** Vetta stays the front door.

**Visual:** Current Club home with Vetta logo and colors visibly integrated. Show role entry for Players, Coaches, Clinics, Leagues, and Tournaments.

**Proof points:**

- A branded public club home and member pathways.
- Staff and member roles connected to the same club.
- A mobile-first experience that can be saved to an iPhone home screen.
- One default club/team/player context that follows the user.

## Slide 4 — Stronger player and coach follow-through

**Headline:** Turn every lesson or clinic into the next useful action.

**Player:** My Lab, Level Up, matchup prep, video review, saved progress, and coach-linked next steps.

**Coach:** Players, training groups, lesson plans, clinics, attendance, assignments, notes, and review.

**Clinic loop:** Create group → invite players → track attendance → assign the next action → review follow-through.

**Value:** Coaches spend less time rebuilding context; players know what to do between sessions.

## Slide 5 — Club competition without another disconnected spreadsheet

**Headline:** Host the competition Vetta wants to offer.

**League and tournament support:** Team leagues, player leagues, flex/singles, doubles, mixed, Tri-Level, Dominant Duo, custom team formats, round robin, pool play, compass, knockout, consolation, ladder, and team tournaments.

**Connected tools:** Invites, eligibility, schedules, shared calendar, availability, lineups, communication, draws, standings, results, rankings, scorecards, recaps, and close-week actions.

**Value:** Vetta can create more member play and clearer staff operations while preserving its existing registration and payment process.

## Slide 6 — Start small. Prove the experience.

**Headline:** A two-week Vetta pilot, no payment required.

**Pilot:** 1 club administrator, 2 coaches, 2 captains, and 10-20 players using one clinic, two team formats, and one league or tournament.

**Success:** First useful action in session one, correct context after return, complete contact import, correct format on every court, clean mobile actions, and no repeated critical blocker.

**Commercial path after the pilot:**

- Club — **$99/month** for one branded club workspace and a practical starter group of staff and member access.
- Club Unlimited — **$199/month** for unlimited coaches and players added through the club.

**Boundary:** Court booking, program registration, and payment processing remain in Vetta's current systems.

**Call to action:** Choose the pilot location and staff owner.

## Presenter note

Do not lead with Captain as a club product. Captain is one connected team workflow inside the Club experience. Lead with the player/coach loop and the ability to host club clinics, leagues, and tournaments; use Captain only to show how a club team moves from roster and availability to lineup, communication, results, recap, and close week.
