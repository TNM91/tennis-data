# Native league and tournament entry refinement

League and tournament browsing now uses the same navy surfaces and discipline accents as the match scorecards. Registration status stays visible above each competition name. Tournament date and venue details have a separate reading area, while connected league entries retain their recorded decision labels.

Entry sheets introduce the registration context before the form. Named fieldsets separate player details, a doubles partner and the confirmations needed before sending. Existing names, self-rating choices, eligibility questions, entry fees notice and organizer approval rules are preserved. Checkbox labels remain full tap targets, and fields wrap at enlarged text sizes.

Saved entry cards distinguish awaiting approval, approved and declined or removed decisions with explicit text and supporting color. A pending request still requires organizer approval; approval awaiting draw placement keeps its separate label. Refresh and lost-confirmation safeguards remain in place.

The change is native presentation only. It adds no dependencies, artwork, endpoints, data collection, scoring rules, access permissions or native signing inputs. The approved brand masters remain unchanged. Browser checks use isolated synthetic entries and cover player and team entry, doubles entry, organizer approval, closed registration, lost replies, stale account responses and phone layouts. Physical iPhone keyboard and VoiceOver acceptance remain separate device checks.
